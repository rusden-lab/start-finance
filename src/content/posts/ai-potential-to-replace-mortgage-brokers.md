## Can AI Replace the ‘Human Touch’ in Mortgage Brokering?

Revolutionising the banking sector, artificial intelligence (AI) has begun to transform the process of mortgage brokering. But the question remains: can AI replace the human touch in mortgage brokering?

To answer this, firstly, let’s understand borrowers’ perspectives. An instrumental part of choosing a mortgage is the reassurance and guidance provided by a human broker. The role of AI in this aspect is still evolving. AI vastly simplifies the mortgage process, makes it faster and more convenient, which encourages borrowers to engage. But, there’s a caveat – the lack of empathy and personal rapport remains a deficiency in AI-driven processes.

To the second question, the importance of the human element in AI-driven mortgages, let’s break it down. Modern AI assistants can streamline specific tasks, generating efficient solutions. However, they can’t yet replicate the nuanced understanding and empathetic communication that human beings bring to the table.

Thus, while a world of robot mortgage brokers isn’t an immediate future scenario, the evolving AI technology will continue to complement human brokers. The consensus leans towards a partnership, where AI handles process efficiency, and humans excel in personalisation and rapport-building. So, in effect, AI can replace certain tasks in mortgage brokering, but not the ‘human touch’.
